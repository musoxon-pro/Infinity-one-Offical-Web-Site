We do not collect any personal information, including but not limited to:

The Game does not save any data locally on your device. Every time you restart the Game, it begins from the default state. No files, preferences, or cache are written to your device.

4. Third-Party Services

The Game does not integrate with any third-party services (social networks, advertising networks, payment gateways, or analytics platforms). All content is self-contained within the Game.

5. Permissions

The Game requests no special permissions on your device. It only requires the standard permissions necessary to run as a game application (e.g., display and touch input), which are automatically granted by the system.

6. Children's Privacy

Since the Game collects no data of any kind, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K. Parents and guardians can rest assured that no information about children is gathered.
